Decoding Cellular Remodeling and Intercellular Signaling in the Lung Cancer Tumor Microenvironment at Single-Cell Resolution



Project Overview



This project investigates cellular composition and remodeling in the lung cancer tumor microenvironment using single-cell RNA sequencing data.



The analysis compares normal lung (nLung) and tumor lung (tLung) samples to explore differences in immune and other cellular populations.



Dataset



\- Source: Gene Expression Omnibus (GEO)

\- Accession: GSE131907

\- Samples analyzed: 22 (11 normal lung and 11 tumor lung)

\- Cells before quality control: 88,144

\- Cells retained after quality control: 86,756



Methods



\- Single-cell RNA-seq data processing

\- Quality control and filtering

\- Gene expression normalization and log transformation

\- Highly variable gene selection

\- Principal Component Analysis (PCA)

\- Cell-type composition analysis

\- Statistical comparison between normal and tumor samples



Preliminary Findings



Initial cell composition analysis suggests changes in immune cell populations between normal and tumor lung tissues, including remodeling of myeloid cell populations.



These findings are exploratory and require further validation.



Tools



Python, Jupyter Notebook, NumPy, Pandas, Scanpy, Matplotlib, SciPy, and h5py.



Project Status



Ongoing research project. Additional dimensionality reduction, clustering, and downstream analyses are planned.
